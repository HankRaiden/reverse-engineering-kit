# After paper trading

The kit deliberately ends at paper trading. It contains **no** code for real orders, and that stays so.

If you move on by yourself afterwards, that is your decision and your responsibility. You should be able to
answer these questions honestly with yes first:

1. Does at least one hypothesis have an **ACCEPT** after Holm correction and walk-forward?
2. Is the paper result, after the pre-registered minimum number of trades, **inside** the backtest confidence
   interval?
3. Does the result stay positive **without the best three trades**?
4. Do you know the **largest drawdown** to expect, and can you sit through it without intervening?
5. Does your exchange hold the stops **on its side**, and do you verify them independently?
6. Does your result come from the **exchange's settlement** and not from your own calculation?
7. Do you start with an amount whose **complete loss** would not change your life?
8. Have you written down **in advance** when you will stop?

If any answer is no, the right next stage is not live, but back to stage 5.

> This kit is not investment advice. Trading with leverage can lead to total loss.

## Mandatory challenger questions for every result
1. Was data before the entry time really only used up to that point (look-ahead)?
2. How many variants were tried in total (including discarded ones)?
3. What is left without the best 3 trades or symbols (concentration)?
4. Does the result hold out of sample (walk-forward)?
5. Are fees, slippage and gaps through the stop accounted for?
6. Would a different, equally plausible period show the opposite?

- **Market:** crypto, spot and perpetual futures; most examples on Bitcoin and large altcoins.
- **Timeframe:** mostly 4h and daily swing trading; separate scalping material exists (not used).
- **What the source does not give:** fixed numeric thresholds for most patterns. Where this example needs a number,
  it is an **own assumption** (stage 3).

  negative result refutes **our rule**, not the trader.

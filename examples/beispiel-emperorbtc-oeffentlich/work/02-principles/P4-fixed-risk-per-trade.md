# P4 · Fix the risk per trade before entering

- **In one sentence:** Decide the stop first and size the position so that hitting the stop costs only a small,
  fixed share of the account.
- **References:** [Risk Management 1](https://x.com/EmperorBTC/status/1334150481820721154) ·
  [Risk Management 2](https://x.com/EmperorBTC/status/1338113899548135424) ·
  [Risk Management (PDF)](https://bit.ly/3pNcnWg)
- **Purpose:** survive losing streaks.
- **Measurable:** yes. Built into every backtest as risk in % of the account (results in R).
- **Importance:** very high.
- **Chairman:** accepted.

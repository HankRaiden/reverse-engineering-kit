# P1 · A breakout only counts with volume

- **In one sentence:** A move out of a range is trustworthy only if volume rises clearly with it; a breakout on thin
  volume is likely a trap.
- **References:** [Price Volume Confirmation](https://x.com/EmperorBTC/status/1420048827684245512) ·
  [Master Class on Volume](https://x.com/EmperorBTC/status/1269720576534470656) ·
  [How to Avoid Fake Breakouts and Traps](https://x.com/EmperorBTC/status/1401219674256207872) ·
  [Price Action Range Breakout](https://x.com/EmperorBTC/status/1405480287828512774)
- **Purpose:** filter out false breakouts.
- **On the chart:** candle closes beyond the recent range; its volume is well above the recent average.
- **Counter-example / caveat:** the source itself also advises beginners to
  [avoid breakout trading](https://x.com/EmperorBTC/status/1401895411946717189) — breakouts are hard.
- **Measurable:** yes (range, close, volume).
- **Importance:** high.
- **Chairman:** accepted.
